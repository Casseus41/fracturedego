Before the door
opens.

You were given a code. It opens one door, once. Read what waits behind it before you use it — the choice you make there is final.

⚠️
Your code works once. The moment you enter it, it’s spent — for you and for anyone else. Don’t use it until you’re ready to finish what you start.

What lies behind it

Three paths. You’ll choose one.

A

The envelope

A sum, sealed by your code. Its length decides what’s inside:

  • A 4-digit code holds $1,000
  • A 10-digit code holds $10,000
  • Delivered the way you choose — bank, CashApp, Zelle, PayPal, Venmo, crypto, cash in hand, or another way
  • No one will ask what it’s for
B

The wish

Ask for something. A favor, a need, a wish — there is no menu.

  • Large or small, practical or personal
  • We do everything within our reach to answer it
  • Some wishes are granted exactly, others in part or in another form. We’ll speak with you before anything moves
  • If what you need is money, choose the envelope
C

The key

Hand it to someone else, one of two ways:

  • We find them. Tell us who they are. We reach them quietly and never say your name unless you ask us to.
  • A new key, shown once. A fresh code of the same value appears on your screen a single time. Copy it, then pass it on with this address.

Either way, your code is spent. If you ask us to reach someone and they decline, we’ll tell you and help you choose again.
