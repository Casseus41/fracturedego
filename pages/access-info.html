What your code opens

After you enter your code, you’ll choose one of these three.

A

A gift of money

A one-time gift. The amount is set by the length of your code:

  • A 4-digit code is worth $1,000 (USD)
  • A 7-digit code is worth $10,000 (USD)
  • Received in US dollars or your local currency, by the method you choose — bank, CashApp, Zelle, PayPal, Venmo, crypto, cash in person, or another way
  • No questions about how you use it

Older 10-digit codes are honored at $10,000.

B

A wish

Ask for something specific — a favor, a need, a wish. There’s no list to choose from.

  • Big or small, practical or personal
  • The fractured Ego team fulfills every wish to the best of our ability
  • Some wishes can be granted exactly; others in part or in a different way. We’ll talk with you before anything happens
  • If what you need is money, choose option A instead
C

Pass it on

Give this opportunity to someone else, in one of two ways:

  • We reach out for you. Tell us who they are and how to reach them. We contact them discreetly and never name you unless you ask us to.

Either way, your current code is used up. If you ask us to reach out and they decline, we’ll email you and help you choose again.
